# Elijah Williams  

ABOUT ELIJAH:

Elijah Williams, an educator and technologist, is the Director of the Robotics and Digital Fabrication Research Labs at the Spitzer School of Architecture, City College of New York | CUNY. His teaching and research work focuses on building performance simulation and both physical and digital modeling complexity.

His work as the founding member of ARTIFEX TOOLS challenged the digital first nature of building assessment   by creating hardware and software tools to improve access to complex systems and data structures. Later, along with Clarke Snell and Vanessa Viola, he was a founding member of the Simulated and Measured Environments Lab at New York Institute of Technology School of Architecture and Design and presented at NYC Open Data week, the IMF and ALA. In Spring 2024, he completed his training to become a Certified Passive House Designer and is always tinkering with an environmental control system.

He attended Cal Poly San Luis Obispo for his NAAB Bachelor of Architecture and his Master of Science in Architectural Technology but is originally from Berkeley, California. He has previously taught at Spitzer CCNY CUNY, Cal Poly SLO, New York Institute of Technology and Boston Architectural College.
